Two gaps to fix before going live: scanned claim forms and Spanish emails

Scanned claim forms

  • 29 of the 40 claimed-amount misses were scanned or handwritten forms
  • The other 11 had more than one amount, or a range
  • Fix: add OCR (Azure AI Document Intelligence) before the AI reads them, then retest

Spanish emails

  • 75.0% routed to the right team, against 94.4% for English
  • Only 60 Spanish emails in the test set
  • Fix: label 200 more Spanish emails, then retest
Both fixes are in the pilot plan, and both get retested before anything goes live. Don't promise a number for the retest: we'll know after the pilot.
