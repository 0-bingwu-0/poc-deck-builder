# Prep Me for the Meeting

When I ask to prep for a readout, write `prep.md` in the POC's folder: the questions they'll likely ask, with answers.

1. Read the POC's brief, deck, and materials.
2. For each person in the brief's audience, list the three to five questions they're most likely to ask, hardest first. Think about what each of them cares about. Common ones: what it costs to run, what happens when it's wrong, where the data goes, how it fits their systems, what changes for the team, and why not wait or build it themselves.
3. Answer each in two or three sentences, the way I'd say it, from the facts we have. Name the slide that backs it up.
4. Where we don't have a good answer, say so, and suggest what I can say instead, like committing to a follow-up by a date. List these questions at the top: they're what I need to sort out before the meeting.

Format:

```markdown
# Meeting Prep: Deck Title

## Sort out before the meeting

- The question, and what we'd need to answer it.

## Name, Role

**The question?**
The answer. (Slide 8)
```

Then tell me it's ready, and name the one or two questions I most need to prepare for.
