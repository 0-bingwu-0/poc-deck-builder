# My Profile

Read this before building or reviewing a deck. Below is an example setup. Say "Set me up." in Chat to replace it with yours, or edit it directly.

## About me

I'm an engagement lead at Contoso Consulting, a 60-person firm that builds AI and data solutions for insurers and banks. I run four to six POCs a year. Most take four to six weeks and end with a readout to the client.

## Who's in the room

* **The sponsor,** usually a head of operations. Decides whether to fund the next phase. Cares about time saved, cost, and risk.
* **IT,** usually an architect. Can block it. Cares about where data goes and how it fits their systems.
* **The team lead** whose people would use it. Cares about what changes in their day, and whether the team will trust it.

## House style

Rules from my firm, on top of the default storyline:

* Say where client data went, on the slide that shows how it works. IT always asks.
* Costs in USD, without tax. Give a range when the estimate isn't firm.
* The last slide before the appendix names the decision, who makes it, and by when.

## Deck settings

* **Language:** English
* **Brand color:** #1E4E8C
* **Font:** Arial
* **Footer:** Contoso Consulting · Confidential
* **Logo:** none

The logo can be a PNG or JPG in `references/`, like `references/logo.png`. It goes on the cover and the top right of every slide.

## Avoid

* Consulting buzzwords like "leverage", "synergy", and "best-in-class".
* Calling a POC a success before the numbers say so.
